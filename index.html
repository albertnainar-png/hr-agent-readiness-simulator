What should remain controlled?

The demo separates AI assistance from accountable decisions and system enforcement.

AI may assist

Identify inconsistencies, summarize known facts, classify an enquiry and draft an escalation note.

Human review

Resolve conflicting records, interpret exceptions and approve consequential decisions.

System enforcement

Authentication, permissions, transaction authorization and audit logging must be enforced outside the model.

A concept for making AI readiness testable at the process level.

This is a synthetic proof of concept, not a production-ready agent or compliance certification. The next step would be validating test cases and controls with the appropriate process, security and governance owners.
